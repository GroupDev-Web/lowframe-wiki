# Important Gameplay Changes

LowFrame changes some normal Minecraft mechanics.

These changes are important because the server may not behave exactly like a normal survival world.

## No Fall Damage in the Overworld

Players do not take fall damage in the Overworld.

Falling from a high location in the Overworld will not remove health.

Do not assume that fall damage is disabled in every world.

The Nether, The End, event areas, and other worlds may use different settings.

## Overworld Mob Spawning

Mobs do not continue spawning normally in the Overworld.

Existing mob types, including villagers, animals, and other passive or neutral mobs, do not naturally spawn in Overworld chunks.

## Hostile Mobs and Newly Loaded Chunks

When an Overworld chunk is loaded for the first time, hostile mobs can spawn during that chunk's first night.

After the first night has passed, no additional hostile mobs will naturally spawn in that chunk.

This means newly explored chunks may contain hostile mobs during their first night, but older loaded chunks should stop producing new hostile mobs.

## Mobs That Are Already Present

Mobs that already exist are not automatically removed.

Mobs may still be present when they are:

- Generated with the world

- Already inside a chunk

- Bred by players

- Moved from another location

- Created by a mob spawner

- Spawned using commands

- Spawned by a plugin or special server feature

The spawning restrictions only control new natural mob spawns.

## Different Worlds May Use Different Settings

Gameplay settings may be different in:

- The Overworld

- The Nether

- The End

- Spawn

- Event areas

- Other server worlds

A feature enabled in one world may be disabled in another.

The Overworld mob-spawning changes described on this page may not apply to the Nether, The End, or special server worlds.

## Plugin-Based Features

LowFrame uses plugins that change or add features such as:

- Teleportation

- Unlimited homes

- Shops

- Economy

- Crates

- Rewards

- Authentication

- Menus

- NPCs

- Version support

- Mob spawning

- Fall damage

When a LowFrame message or wiki page conflicts with normal Minecraft behavior, follow the LowFrame information.

## Reporting Unexpected Behavior

Contact LowFrame staff when:

- Fall damage occurs in the Overworld.

- Passive or neutral mobs naturally begin spawning in the Overworld.

- Hostile mobs continue naturally spawning in a chunk after its first night.

- A teleport places you somewhere unsafe.

- A menu removes or duplicates items.

- A shop creates an incorrect balance.

- A command suddenly stops working.

Include:

- Your Minecraft username

- Your platform

- Your Minecraft version

- The LowFrame server you were using

- The world where it happened

- The approximate location

- What happened before the problem
