# Economy, Shops, and Rewards

LowFrame uses an economy for shops, payments, rewards, crates, and other server features.

# Economy

## Check Your Balance

Run:

`/balance`

Common aliases include:

`/bal`

`/money`

## Pay Another Player

Run:

`/pay <player> <amount>`

Example:

`/pay ExamplePlayer 500`

Check the username and amount before sending money.

Payments may not be reversible.

## Balance Leaderboard

Run:

`/baltop`

This displays the richest-player leaderboard.

## Wallet

Run:

`/wallet`

The wallet displays your available currencies.

# Server Shop

## Open the Shop

Run:

`/shop`

Select a category to view available items.

Open a specific section with:

`/shop <section>`

Example:

`/shop blocks`

## Buying Items

To buy an item:

1. Open `/shop`.

2. Select a category.

3. Select an item.

4. Choose an amount.

5. Confirm the purchase.

A purchase may fail when:

- You do not have enough money.

- Buying is disabled.

- Your inventory is full.

- The item is unavailable.

## Selling Items

Open the selling menu:

`/sellgui`

Place supported items inside the menu.

Items without a sell price will not be sold.

## Sell All Commands

Sell supported inventory items:

`/sellall inventory`

Sell items matching the stack in your hand:

`/sellall hand`

Sell a selected item type:

`/sellall <item>`

Example:

`/sellall cobblestone`

# Rewards

## Rewards Menu

Run:

`/rewards`

Rewards may include:

- Daily rewards

- Money

- Items

- Crate keys

- Temporary bonuses

- Event rewards

Some rewards have a cooldown before they can be claimed again.

## Battle Pass

Run:

`/battlepass`

The battle pass may include:

- Challenges

- Progress levels

- Seasonal goals

- Free rewards

- Other unlockable rewards

Battle-pass progress may reset when a season ends.

# Crates

Crates give a randomly selected reward.

A crate may require:

- A physical key

- A virtual key

- Money

- Another opening requirement

Preview a crate:

`/crates preview <crate>`

Example:

`/crates preview vote`

Check virtual keys:

`/crates key inspect`

## Physical Keys

Physical keys are inventory items.

They can usually be:

- Stored

- Traded

- Dropped

- Lost

## Virtual Keys

Virtual keys are stored on your account.

They do not normally appear in your inventory.

# Problems

Contact staff when:

- A shop removes money without giving the item.

- Selling removes an item without paying you.

- A reward cannot be claimed after its cooldown.

- A crate key disappears without opening a crate.

- A menu duplicates items or money.
